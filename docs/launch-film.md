# KEPT — launch films

Scripts and storyboards for the consumer launch. Four pieces: a teaser, the hero film, a
product film, and cutdowns. Everything on screen is listed in §6 so copy can be approved in
one pass.

Brand reference: [docs/design/kept-design-system.md](design/kept-design-system.md). Voice is
warm, plain, short. Never celebrate portfolio size; only celebrate showing up.

---

## 0. The three rules

Every piece obeys these. They are not stylistic preferences, they are the product's own
privacy model applied to the camera.

1. **No numbers.** No balance, no return, no chart, ever. If a figure would be legible, we are
   out of focus or off-angle.
2. **No screens** — until the last five seconds of the hero film. Phones are face-down,
   pocketed, or held past the frame edge. We hear the notification and we see the face.
3. **The miss stays in.** The year in these films contains three broken promises and the films
   say so out loud. Nobody else in this category admits failure, which is exactly why it lands.

The one-line pitch for press: *a film about an investing app with no money in it.*

## 1. Hero film — "Fifty-Two Sundays"

**90 seconds · 2.39:1 · 35mm anamorphic (anchors) + 16mm handheld (travel)**

**Logline.** A year of one small promise, told in the faces of four people keeping it — and in
the silence when one of them doesn't.

### Cast

Four people who are already in each other's circle, though the film withholds that until Act
III. Real faces, specific lives, deliberately not young-professional-in-a-loft.

| | Who | Where they keep it |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | Night-shift nurse, late 40s | Her kitchen, coming off shift at dawn |
| **B** | Rideshare driver, 30s | Parked car, 2am, city glow through the windscreen |
| **C** | Grandmother, 60s | A doorway with the monsoon behind it |
| **D** | Warehouse picker, early 20s | A bus shelter, headphones on |

### Typography on screen

Week supers only — `WEEK 01`, bottom-left, Sora ExtraBold, small, cream at 70%. They are the
film's only type until the cards, and they do the work that would otherwise need narration.
**No location supers.** This should feel like one world, not a travelogue.

### Script

| Time | Picture | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00–0:04** | Black. `WEEK 01` fades up, holds, fades. | One cup set down on wood. Room tone underneath — a fridge, a car idling, rain. |
| 0:04–0:08 | **A.** Kitchen, first light. She is still in scrubs. She sets a cup down, picks up a phone we never see the front of, and her thumb moves once. She exhales and looks out of the window. | Her cup. Clean, single. |
| 0:08–0:12 | **B.** Parked car, 2am, sodium light. Same gesture, one-handed, steering wheel in frame. | His cup — lower pitch, car ambience around it. |
| 0:12–0:16 | **C.** Doorway, monsoon behind her. Same gesture. She smiles faintly at the rain. A small pair of child's sandals sits by the door — the granddaughter is in the film without being in the frame. | Her cup, plus torrential rain on leaves and a tin roof. |
| 0:16–0:20 | **D.** Bus shelter, headphones. Thumb. He doesn't look up. | His cup, muffled through the headphones. |
| **0:20–0:30** | **The year starts moving.** Same four setups, cut faster and faster. Light swings across the same walls. Rain, then not. Different mugs, different hair. Twelve Sundays in ten seconds. Supers flick past: `04` `07` `11` `14`. | The four cups begin to interlock into a rhythm. A low warm pad starts underneath, built from the same recording stretched. |
| **0:30–0:36** | `WEEK 19`. **B's car. The driver's seat is empty.** Streetlight, rain on the windscreen, nobody. Hold. Hold past comfortable. | **Silence where his cup should be.** The pad thins. Only rain. |
| 0:36–0:40 | `WEEK 26`. The seat again. Still empty. Shorter this time, which is worse. | Nothing. The pad drops out entirely. |
| **0:40–0:45** | **A**, at her kitchen table. She stops mid-gesture. She is looking at something off-frame, and her face changes. She does one small thing with her thumb — deliberate, not routine. | A single soft tone, different from the cups. The only non-cup sound in the film. |
| **0:45–0:50** | `WEEK 27`. **B is back in the car.** Same framing as 0:08. He sits a second before he does it. | **His cup returns** — and it is warmer in the mix than it was, closer, less ambience. The pad comes back with it. |
| **0:50–1:02** | Full montage, all four, seasons pushing through windows. Rooms accrue life: a plant gets bigger, a photograph appears, the granddaughter is visibly taller. Once or twice, two of them appear in the same cut for the first time. Supers: `31` `36` `40` `45`. | The rhythm fills out. Four distinct cup-pitches now audible as separate voices. |
| **1:02–1:10** | Cutting tightens to single frames of each. The four gestures become one gesture. | Near-melodic. The four pitches trading. |
| **1:10–1:20** | `WEEK 52`. All four, in sequence, unhurried — back to Act I's rhythm but everything in the rooms has changed. A, then B, then C, then D. | Four cups, in sequence — **then landing together as a chord.** The only simultaneous moment in the film. |
| **1:20–1:25** | **The first screen in the film.** Macro, shallow: a single ring closing. Nothing else on the display is in focus. | The chord decays. |
| **1:25–1:30** | Cards on ink, cream type. | Silence, then one last cup on the logo. |

### End cards

```
Fifty-two promises.
Forty-nine kept.

KEPT
Promises compound.
```

Three beats, roughly 1.5s each. "Forty-nine" is the whole film — do not let anyone talk you
out of it in the edit.

### Direction

- **Camera at seated eye height, always.** No drone, no crane, no push-in. You are with these
  people, not observing them. The film is small on purpose.
- **Practical light only** — windows, lamps, a dashboard, a phone lighting a face from below.
- The four anchor setups are locked-off and identical every time. Only the travel shots move.
- **The empty seat at 0:30 is the most important shot in the film.** It needs to be boring for
  long enough to be uncomfortable. Shoot it at three lengths and find it in the edit.

## 2. Score brief

**The entire score is one sound: a ceramic cup set down on a wooden table.**

Record fifty-two takes on four different cups and four different tables — one per cast member,
so each person has a pitch. Everything else in the arrangement is those recordings stretched,
pitched, reversed and layered into pads. No other instruments.

The arc: single and dry in Act I → interlocking rhythm through Act II → pad drops out entirely
at the miss → returns warmer and closer on the return → four voices trading by Act III → a
major chord at week 52.

Two consequences worth taking seriously:

- **The app's confirmation sound should be this sound**, so the film and the product share a
  voice literally rather than thematically.
- **Release the score as a track** with a note explaining what it is. A film scored entirely
  with one cup is a story journalists will run on its own.

Reference for the pad texture: Ólafur Arnalds, Hania Rani. Warm, close-mic'd, unhurried.

## 3. Teaser — "The Sound"

**20 seconds · drops one week before the hero film · no logo until 0:17**

| Time | Picture | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:03 | Black. | One cup. Silence after it. |
| 0:03–0:12 | Black. A small cream counter, centre: `01` … ticking up. It climbs unevenly — sometimes a jump, sometimes a pause. | One cup per number. The rhythm builds. |
| 0:12–0:15 | The counter stalls at `19`. Holds. | **Silence.** Three full seconds of it. |
| 0:15–0:17 | It resumes: `20` … accelerating to `52`. | Cups return, faster, warmer, resolving to the chord. |
| 0:17–0:20 | `KEPT` — cream on ink. Then: `Promises compound.` | The chord decays. |

No product, no explanation, no URL. The stall at 19 is the entire teaser.

## 4. Product film — "How it works"

**60 seconds · 16:9 · screens allowed, numbers allowed, no people**

The counterpart to the hero film: here we show the mechanic. Rendered rather than shot —
`web/components/rings.tsx` draws the real ring geometry and `web/lib/year.ts` holds the same
52-week dataset the landing page uses, so the film is the product rather than a mockup of it.

| Time | Picture | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:06 | Three rings draw from empty on cream. | `Promises compound.` |
| 0:06–0:16 | **Promise.** A weekly amount is set. The promise ring appears empty, waiting. | `Pick an amount. Say it out loud.` · `$20 a week` |
| 0:16–0:28 | **Invest.** The buy ticket: $20 in, a real quote out, a mint verified. Live Jupiter numbers, not props. | `Tokenised equities and private-market names.` · `Settled on Solana, from your own wallet.` |
| 0:28–0:40 | **Circle.** Four ring-glyphs. Three close. One doesn't — a nudge goes out. | `Four people who notice when you keep it.` |
| 0:40–0:50 | **The year.** The 52-week scrub, compressed: promise ring sawtoothing, goal ring climbing, three red bars in the strip. | `52 promises · 49 kept · $980 in` |
| 0:50–0:56 | The privacy flip: the card loses three rows, the ring grows. | `Your circle sees the ring. Not the number.` |
| 0:56–1:00 | Widget on a home screen, then the wordmark. | `KEPT` · `Promises compound.` |

Captions burned in. 85% of this will be watched muted.

## 5. Cutdowns

- **6s vertical, ×4** — one person, one Sunday, one cup, one card. Cast A/B/C/D.
- **15s vertical** — Act II only: the rhythm, the empty seat, the return. This is the strongest
  short cut because the silence works even on a phone speaker.
- **The Circle, 4 × 30s** — each cast member's own year, cut from the same material. Releases
  weekly after launch and gives the campaign a month of legs.

## 7. Notes for production

- A real year cannot be shot for a launch. Either build the four spaces on stage and dress them
  fourteen ways, or shoot four practical locations over four days with controlled seasonal
  dressing and exterior backings. Four days, four cast, 35mm, one composer.
- Cast for faces that hold a locked-off frame doing nothing. Three of the four best shots in
  this film are someone not speaking.
- Budget the silence. At 0:30 and 0:36 there is nothing on the soundtrack but rain, and that is
  the moment the film either works or doesn't.

**If produced with AI generation rather than shot**, see
[launch-film-prompts.md](launch-film-prompts.md) — the full Veo 3.1 prompt package, and the
three constraints that changed this script.
