# KEPT — announcement film, shot by shot

**"People who notice" · 60 seconds · 16:9, reframed for 9:16 · for the beta launch**

> **Superseded.** Casting and keeping four people consistent across generated footage was more
> than the launch needed; the launch video became the motion-graphics
> [product-video.md](product-video.md). Kept for the shot list and the copy.

The fifth piece, alongside the four in [launch-film.md](launch-film.md). It borrows the
*grammar* of Apple's Fitness+ launch film (2020) — not its words, not its shots — and
puts KEPT's own product, cast, cup score and three rules through it.

---

## What we took from the reference, and what we refused

The reference was studied frame by frame: 62 seconds, 65 cuts. Five techniques carry it.

| Technique in the reference | KEPT's version |
|---|---|
| **Bookended by one face.** A long, still close-up opens it and another closes it; everything between is fast. The slow ends are what make the fast middle feel human. | Opens on **A**, the nurse, closes on **D**, the warehouse picker — two of the four from the hero film. |
| **Words as the script.** One or two words per card, black on flat grey, cut to the music, with the size jumping between words. | One or two words per card, **ink on cream**, landing on the **cup hits** of the score (launch-film.md §2). Sora for the words; Instrument Serif italic for the one accent word per line. |
| **Faces as "everyone".** Half-second bursts of different people looking straight into the lens. | Faces looking into the lens *are* the product: people who notice. Eight of them, in two bursts. |
| **The product in the room, not as screenshots.** | A phone on a kitchen table, the widget on a home screen at a bus stop, a nudge arriving. |
| **The rings held back as the climax.** Colour saturates, the rings grow until they fill the frame with words in the centre, then shrink onto the device. | Kiwi, violet, coral fill the frame; *Promises compound.* sits in the centre; they shrink onto the home-screen widget. |

**What we refused.** The reference shows a heart rate, a timer or a calorie count in half its
shots. KEPT's version never shows a figure about money — that is rule 1, and it is the whole
point of the product. The reference also cuts as fast as five times a second. We don't: the
cup score needs room between hits, and a brand about *slowly* can't be edited like a
trailer. About 40 cuts, not 65.

### The three rules, applied

1. **No numbers** — kept. The only figures on screen are week numbers. The rings appear; no
   amount, balance or price ever does. The buy screen is not in this film.
2. **No screens until the last five seconds** — relaxed, as the product film relaxes it.
   Screens appear from 0:16, but only ever the rings, the widget and a nudge. If a number
   would be legible, it is out of focus or off the frame.
3. **The miss stays in** — kept. Week 19, the empty driver's seat from the hero film, and the
   return.

## Type on screen

- **Card:** `--background` cream, full frame. Ink type, never pure black.
- **Words:** Sora, weight 700–800. Size changes between cards on purpose — a small word, then a
  large one — but never inside a card.
- **Accent:** one word per line in Instrument Serif italic (*notice*, *how much*, *compound*). As
  on the site, full stops sit inside the accent.
- **Timing:** every card lands *on* a cup hit, never between. The edit is cut to the score, not
  the other way round.
- **Week supers:** as in the hero film — small, bottom-left, Sora ExtraBold, cream at 70%.

## Colour

Natural, warm and a little underexposed for Acts I–IV — the same practical-light world as the
hero film. Act V is the only saturated passage, graded to the ring colours: kiwi `#A3E635`,
violet `#7C5CFF`, coral `#FF6B4A`. It returns to natural for the final face, so the colour
reads as a moment, not a style.

## Making it

- **Live action** is the hero film's four cast and locations, plus four faces for the bursts.
  Everything live is shootable in the hero film's four days — this is mostly pickups from
  those setups, framed tighter.
- **Everything rendered** comes from the product's own code: the rings from the same geometry
  the app and the site draw (`web/components/rings.tsx`), the award objects from the app's 3D
  set, the type from the design tokens. The film is the product rather than a picture of it.
- **Phone screens** are composited, never shot live, so no stray figure can be legible.
- **If generated rather than shot**, the face and car shots follow the prompt package in
  [launch-film-prompts.md](launch-film-prompts.md); the rendered shots don't change.

## Cutdowns

- **15s** — shots 1, 28–29, 30–32. One face, the rings, one face, the logo.
- **6s vertical** — shot 28 alone, then 31. The rings filling the frame is the one image that
  works with the sound off.
- **9:16 reframe** — faces are already centred; cards re-set in a narrower column; the sweeping
  type in shot 24 runs as three lines stacked instead of one.
